What to judge

  • Can you read Cla and Cdx at 16 px without consulting the enlarged view?
  • Does A/O remain recognizable after swapping positions?
  • Can you distinguish 32% from 78% with colors removed?
  • Does the stale mark remain visible at high utilization?
  • Bars alone are a comparison baseline: they do not meet the provider-recognition requirement when moved independently.

What this does and does not prove

  • 16/20/24/32 are raster dimensions, shown at the same number of CSS pixels at 100% browser zoom. They are samples, not a verified mapping to Windows scaling settings.
  • Labels use a tiny hand-drawn bitmap alphabet, avoiding dependence on macOS font rendering. This is a rendering experiment, not a new app font.
  • Stale: violet plus a corner marker. Error: exclamation mark. Missing: dash. Disabled: slash. Connecting: three dots. Identity stays present in A and B.
  • Percentages are in tooltips here. Optional visible percentages need a separate space/readability decision on Windows.
  • Real Windows checks still need to cover OS scaling, taskbar appearance, hidden/reordered icons, tooltip behavior, hit targets, and popover anchoring.
