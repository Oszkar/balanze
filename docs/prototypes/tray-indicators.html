Content decisions to judge

  • Cl / Cx: short and distinct, but learned abbreviations. A single C is ambiguous.
  • Provider identity: letterforms in paired icons support icon-only mode. Shared bars rely on left/right position and are weaker for first-time users.
  • One percentage per provider: highest utilization, not always the five-hour window. Tooltip names the selected window.
  • Unavailable is not 0%: a dash means no value; a dotted outline means connecting; a slash means disabled. The tooltip supplies the full state.

Proposed behavior, not approved

  • Fresh quota uses the existing rounded 50/75/90 color thresholds.
  • Stale quota retains its last fill in violet and uses “~” before its percentage. This is a last-known value, not a forecast.
  • Separate indicators isolate a provider failure. Combined mode warns when a participating provider is stale or failing.
  • A billing or JSONL-only failure stays in the popover, without recoloring healthy quota indicators.
  • Combined mode keeps today's Codex tie priority. A stale candidate is never silently discarded to make the combined view look healthy.
